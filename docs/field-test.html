Gaps to confirm

Gaps we found in the code. Check whether each one actually hurts in a real shop. These become your "changes needed" slide.

Sales only work by scanning

New Order has no product search. Loose items, fruit and vegetables, and items with damaged barcodes can't be billed.

Stock In always asks for batch and expiry

Good for pharmacies, slow for kiranas. Should it be optional depending on shop type?

No bill for the customer

There's no WhatsApp or printed receipt. Ask whether customers want one.

No returns, deletes or edits to past sales

A mistaken sale can't be undone. Find out how often billing mistakes happen.

English-only screens

Check whether the owner or their staff would prefer Hindi or Marathi.

One phone per shop

If two people bill at the same time on two phones, the last edit wins. Ask whether more than one person bills.

Ask the shopkeeper
  1. How do you track sales, stock and credit today? (Show us the notebook.)
  2. What takes up most of your time every day?
  3. Which screen would you use daily, and which ones never?
  4. What was confusing or slow?
  5. What's missing that would make you use this instead of paper?
  6. Would you pay for this? How much per month feels fair?
  7. Who else in the shop would use it, and how comfortable are they with phones?
  8. How many products do you stock, and how many have barcodes?
